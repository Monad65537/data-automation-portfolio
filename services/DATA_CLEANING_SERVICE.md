# Excel & CSV Data Cleaning, Validation, and Transformation

**Service status:** Proposed introductory freelance offering  
**Specialization:** Structured spreadsheet data processing  
**Tools:** Python, pandas, openpyxl, automated validation

## Service Overview

I help businesses prepare structured Excel and CSV data for reporting, import, and analysis.

The service focuses on consistent formatting, agreed validation rules, traceable handling of problematic records, and reproducible output files.

The original dataset is preserved during processing.

## Available Services

- Standardizing column names and text formatting.
- Removing unwanted leading and trailing whitespace.
- Normalizing supported dates and numeric representations.
- Validating required identifiers and field values.
- Detecting duplicates according to agreed business rules.
- Separating valid and problematic records where appropriate.
- Converting between supported CSV and XLSX representations.
- Performing explicitly specified transformations.
- Combining compatible datasets where the mapping and merge rules are agreed.
- Producing a summary of transformations and validation findings.

## Scope and Exclusions

This offering does not automatically include:

- Recovery of data already lost in upstream software.
- Manual research to reconstruct missing business information.
- Guaranteed preservation of original Excel formatting, formulas, macros, or pivot tables.
- Data scraping from websites.
- Database administration or deployment.
- Financial auditing or accounting certification.
- Automatic resolution of semantically ambiguous duplicates.
- Unlimited custom programming.
- Processing files outside the agreed formats and schemas.
- Handling confidential or regulated personal data without an approved data-handling arrangement.

## Important Data Rules

Identifiers such as `000123` are treated as text when their exact formatting matters.

Duplicate identifiers are not automatically grounds for deletion. Their meaning must be established with the client.

Invalid or ambiguous records are retained for review when required by the agreed processing rules.

No record is silently removed merely to produce a cleaner-looking dataset.

## Acceptance Criteria

A project is ready for delivery when:

1. Agreed transformation rules are implemented.
2. Output schema and file format match the specification.
3. Required data integrity checks pass.
4. Rejected or unresolved records are documented.
5. Results are verified using representative examples and applicable automated checks.
6. The delivery summary accurately records what was changed and what remains unresolved.

## Client Responsibilities

The client must provide authorized access to the data, describe the desired output, specify applicable business rules, and review unresolved ambiguities.

Changes to agreed requirements are evaluated before implementation.
